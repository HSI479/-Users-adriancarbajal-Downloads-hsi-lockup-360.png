# Pre-launch Shorts plan: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook

> **Decision update, Oct 8:** one YouTube channel, **Sharpening Ministries** (`@SharpeningMinistries`,
> Brand Account owned by `media@sharpeningministries.com`), with the 4:19 Podcast as a podcast playlist
> on it. The separate `@419podcast` channel is optional and only to hold the handle. Where a doc below
> says "4:19 channel", read "the podcast playlist on the ministry channel".

Goal: by the morning episode 1 goes live, the people most likely to watch it already know the name
"4:19 Podcast", have seen Larry's face and Mark Carter's, and are subscribed or following where
the episode will appear. Shorts do that job; they do not need to go viral.

Calendar below assumes launch **Tuesday Oct 27**. Everything shifts with the date.

## 1. Where each Short goes and why

| Platform | Account | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | **4:19 Podcast channel** (not the ministry channel) | A subscriber earned by a Short must land on the channel where the episode will publish. The channel exists from Oct 13 with art, About and trailer so it does not look empty. |
| Instagram Reels | `@sharpeningministries` | The ministry's existing followers are the warmest audience. Posting as 4:19 from a zero-follower account would reach nobody. |
| Facebook Reels + feed | Sharpening Ministries Page | Oldest and most link-friendly audience; FB allows the YouTube link in the post text. |

Post **natively** on each platform (upload the file, never share a link to another platform's
video). Export one 1080×1920 master per Short with burned-in captions and no platform watermark;
Instagram down-ranks videos carrying a TikTok or YouTube watermark.

## 2. The window: 2 weeks, 7 pieces, three types

| Type | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook clip** | 4 | 25–45 s from episodes 1–3: a guest line or a story beat, cut *before* the resolution, ending on the launch date card. |
| **Larry to camera** | 2 | 20–30 s in the studio, on the ATEM: "Here's what 4:19 is and when it starts" (Short 1) and "Here's who's on the first episode and why" (Short 6). Record both in one 30-minute session. |
| **Behind the scenes** | 1 | 15–25 s: the studio, the mics, the three men laughing between takes, Larry setting a chair. Human, no pitch. |

Rule for picking hook clips: the first second must make a man stop scrolling. A question, a
confession or a claim; never "On this episode we talk about". OpusClip's virality score is a
starting point only; Michael and Adrian choose by ear.

## 4. Per-platform mechanics

**YouTube Shorts (4:19 channel)**
- Title is the hook sentence, under 60 characters, no "Ep. 1" and no "#shorts".
- Description: the 136-character short bio, then "First episode premieres Tue Oct 27", then the website.
- Related video: the Ep 1 Premiere (from Oct 20). Before that, the channel trailer.
- Schedule all of them in one sitting in Studio (upload as Private, set "Schedule").
- Pin a comment on each: "Subscribe so you don't miss episode 1 on Oct 27."

**Instagram Reels (`@sharpeningministries`)**
- Caption: first line is the hook (that is all that shows in the grid); then one sentence on what
  4:19 is; then "First episode Oct 27 on YouTube. Link in bio." Three to five hashtags
  (`#419podcast #sharpeningministries #mensministry #christianmen #discipleship`).
- Cover frame: a clean face, not a mid-word mouth. Use the thumbnail concept's type if you add a
  cover title (Rokkitt, gold word).
- Also share each Reel to Stories with the "countdown" sticker (set to Oct 27, 6 am) on 1, 4 and 6.
- Link in bio points to `sharpeningministries.com/podcast`, a page on the ministry site that lists
  YouTube, Instagram and Facebook and embeds the trailer. Use the site, not a link-in-bio service.
- Tag Mark Carter's and The King's Refuge's accounts on Shorts 2, 6, 7 so they can reshare.

**Facebook (Sharpening Ministries Page)**
- Post the same file as a Reel, and on 1, 6 and 8 also as a normal feed video post with the
  YouTube link in the text (Facebook keeps links; Instagram strips them).
- Caption can be longer: two or three sentences, the date, the link.
- Ask Larry and the men on the episode to share the Page post to their personal profiles the day
  it goes up; on Facebook that is the single biggest reach lever a small page has.
- Schedule Instagram and Facebook together in Meta Business Suite → Planner.

## 6. What to watch and the one decision it drives

Check Thursday Oct 16 and Thursday Oct 23:

- YouTube Studio → Shorts analytics: **"Viewed vs. swiped away"** and **subscribers from Shorts**.
- Instagram Insights: reach and **follows from the Reel**.
- Facebook: shares.

Decision rule: whichever type (hook clip, Larry to camera, BTS) earns the most follows per view by
Oct 23 becomes 2 of the 3 Shorts in launch week. Do not chase views; chase follows and subscribes.

## 7. After launch

Three Shorts per week per platform from the live episode (Mon/Wed/Fri), each with the full episode
as its related video on YouTube and "link in bio" on Instagram. One of the three each week is
evergreen (a principle, not an episode reference). Larry's solo Shorts on the ministry channel keep
their own rhythm and are not counted here.
