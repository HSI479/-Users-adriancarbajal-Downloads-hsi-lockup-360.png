## One email or two? One.

One Google account can own many Brand Account channels, and that is exactly what Brand Accounts
are for. The separation the team wants (different names, art, audiences, analytics) comes from
having two Brand Accounts, not from having two email addresses. A second sign-in would only add a
second password to lose and a second recovery chain to maintain, with nothing gained.

**Create `media@sharpeningministries.com` and make it the Primary Owner of both channels.** Keep
`larry@sharpeningministries.com` as an **Owner** on both. Reasons to use a role address rather than
Larry's own:

- It survives people. If a producer changes or Larry steps back, nobody has to hand over a personal inbox.
- It is the recovery point for the channels, so it should not be an inbox that also handles
  personal mail, newsletters and password resets for other services.
- Larry still has full control as Owner, and the account switcher shows both channels under
  whichever sign-in he uses.

What `media@` has to be:

- A **real Google account that can sign in**, not an alias or a group. If the domain's email is
  on Google Workspace, add `media@` as a user (one more seat). If the domain's email is on
  Microsoft 365 or another host, create it there, then at `accounts.google.com/signup` choose
  **"Use my current email address instead"** and enter `media@sharpeningministries.com`.
- Recovery phone = a ministry phone (not a personal cell that could change). Recovery email =
  `larry@sharpeningministries.com`. 2-Step Verification on, with backup codes printed and kept
  in the office. Store the password in a shared vault (1Password or Bitwarden), never a text thread.

Roles across both channels, once `media@` exists:

| Person / account | Brand Account role (myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts) | YouTube Studio role |
|---|---|---|
| `media@` | **Primary owner** (after the 7-day wait) | Owner |
| Larry (`larry@`) | Owner | Owner |
| Adrian | Owner while producing (gives a second recovery path) | Owner |
| Michael | — | Editor |

## Order of operations (this changes launch day slightly)

1. Create `media@` first, today if possible. The 7-day clock for Primary Owner starts when it
   accepts an Owner invitation, so the sooner it exists the sooner handoff can happen.
2. Create both channels **signed in as `media@`** using "Use a custom name". Then `media@` is
   Primary Owner from minute one and there is no transfer to do at all. Invite Larry and Adrian as
   Owners and Michael as Editor the same day. This is simpler than creating under Adrian and
   transferring later; the original checklist's handoff section is only needed if `media@` cannot
   exist before the channels do.
3. If Larry already has a YouTube channel under `larry@`, check whether it is a personal channel or
   a Brand Account (Studio → Settings → Channel → Advanced settings → "Move channel to a brand
   account"). A personal channel can be moved to a Brand Account, which makes it `@sharpeningministries`
   with its history intact rather than starting over.
4. Verify the phone on each channel separately (Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility).
   One phone number can verify two channels per year, which is exactly two.

## Meta (Instagram and Facebook)

Same principle: one ministry-owned **Meta Business Portfolio** (business.facebook.com), admin
`media@`, with Larry and Adrian as admins too.

- **Facebook Page:** Sharpening Ministries (existing page if there is one; claim it into the portfolio).
- **Instagram:** `@sharpeningministries` as a Professional (Creator or Business) account, connected
  to the Page so Reels can be scheduled to both from Meta Business Suite in one step.
- **`@419podcast` on Instagram:** reserve it now (a second professional account can be added to
  the same phone/email), but **post from `@sharpeningministries` through launch.** A second
  Instagram account splits a small audience in half; the podcast gets its own account only when it
  has momentum of its own. Same handle reservation on TikTok and X, same posture.

## Cross-promotion between the two YouTube channels

- Each channel lists the other in Customization → Layout → Featured channels.
- The ministry channel's home page gets a "4:19 Podcast" section pulling the podcast playlist (a
  playlist from another channel can be featured as a section).
- Larry's solo Shorts on the ministry channel carry the 4:19 episode as their related video when
  the topic matches; 4:19 episode descriptions link the ministry channel's teaching playlist.
- The 4:19 Shorts live on the 4:19 channel, not the ministry channel, so the subscribers they
  earn land where the episodes publish (see the Shorts plan).
