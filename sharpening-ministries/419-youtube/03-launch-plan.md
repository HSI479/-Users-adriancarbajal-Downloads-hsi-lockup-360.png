Status: draft for Adrian's review. Dates below assume a **Tuesday, October 27, 2026 launch**;
nothing else in this plan changes if the date moves, only the calendar. The three episodes in hand:

| Ep | Working title | Recorded | On mic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bold & Courageous | Sep 29, 2026 | Larry with Mark Carter, founder of The King's Refuge |
| 2 | Six Months Later, Part 1 | Oct 1, 2026 | Larry with Taylor, Walker and Adrian |
| 3 | Six Months Later, Part 2 | Oct 1, 2026 | Larry with Taylor, Walker and Adrian |

## 1. Upload order and calendar

**Lead with "Bold & Courageous."** It has a guest with his own network (Mark Carter will share it,
and The King's Refuge may repost), a single clear theme, and a title that works cold for someone who
has never heard of Sharpening. "Six Months Later" is a better *second* act: once a viewer knows who
Larry is, the story of three men six months after a Hot Springs weekend is the proof of the
mission statement. Running the two parts on consecutive weeks keeps that story together.

Alternative if Larry prefers to open with the ministry's own men: 1 = Six Months Later Pt 1,
2 = Pt 2, 3 = Bold & Courageous. Everything below still applies; swap the numbers.

| Date (Tue) | What goes live | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 13 | Teaser window opens: Short #1 | Channel is already public with art, About, trailer and the (empty) podcast playlist |
| Oct 13 → Oct 26 | 6 teaser Shorts (Mon/Wed/Fri) | Two from each episode, see §5 |
| Oct 27, 6:00 am CT | **Ep 1 Bold & Courageous** as a scheduled Premiere | Premiere gives a countdown page to link from Shorts and socials a week early |
| Oct 27 → Nov 2 | 3 Shorts from Ep 1 | Each linked to the full episode as its "related video" |
| Nov 3, 6:00 am CT | **Ep 2 Six Months Later, Pt 1** | Normal scheduled publish (not a Premiere) |
| Nov 10, 6:00 am CT | **Ep 3 Six Months Later, Pt 2** | Normal scheduled publish |
| Nov 10 onward | Weekly Tuesday slot holds; record Ep 4–6 by Nov 3 to keep a 3-episode buffer | Buffer rule from the Sep 30 meeting |

Upload all three episodes as **Private** on the same day the channel is created so processing,
thumbnails, chapters, end screens and the playlist can be finished in one sitting; then schedule.
Uploads over 15 minutes and custom thumbnails need the phone verification step first
(checklist, step 4).

## 4. Thumbnails

Rendered concepts are in `02-channel-art/out/` (the pipeline is in `02-channel-art/README.md`).
All three share the system: charcoal field, a short gold rule and an Archivo episode tag top-left,
a 2–4 word Rokkitt title with the key word in gold, a one-line Libre Franklin subtitle naming the
people, the medallion and "4:19 PODCAST" bottom-left. Text stays in the left 55% so a face can
carry the right side and so the 168 px phone thumbnail still reads.

| Ep | Concept | Image to supply |

## 5. Shorts: teaser window and post-launch cadence

Source: the shorts already cut with the local pipeline (`sharpening-ministries/419-shorts/`) and
the OpusClip batch. Pick by one rule: **does the first second make a man stop scrolling.** Vertical
9:16, 1080×1920, under 60 s (up to 3 min is allowed, but teasers should stay 25–45 s), captions
burned in, the 9:16 logo sting (`assets/419/out/`) as the last 1.5 s, no music bed under speech.

**Teaser window (Oct 13–26): 6 Shorts, Mon/Wed/Fri at 7:00 am CT.**

| # | Date | From | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon Oct 13 | Ep 1 | Mark's strongest line about courage; caption ends "Full episode Oct 27" |
| 2 | Wed Oct 15 | Ep 2 | One of the three men on what changed first after Hot Springs |
| 3 | Fri Oct 17 | Ep 3 | The "what stuck" answer, cut before the punchline |

Every teaser Short: title = the hook sentence (no "Ep. 1", no "#shorts" in the title), description

**After launch: 3 Shorts per episode week (Mon/Wed/Fri), then 2 per week once the backlog is gone.**
Each Short's related video is the full episode it came from. One Short per week should be
"evergreen" (a principle, not a reference to the episode) so it keeps working months later.

Also on Oct 13: upload the channel trailer (sting + 45–60 s montage from all three) so a new visitor
who arrives from a Short sees what the show is.

## 6. Podcast playlist

YouTube treats a podcast as a playlist flagged as a podcast; that flag is what lists the show in
YouTube Music and in the Podcasts tab.

1. Studio → Create → **New podcast** → "Create a new podcast".
2. Title `4:19 Podcast`. Description: the 740-character About text. Visibility Public.
3. Square thumbnail: `02-channel-art/out/podcast-cover-3000x3000.jpg`.
4. Add the three episode videos in order (Ep 1 first). Full episodes only; Shorts never go in it.
5. Set the podcast playlist as the first section on the channel home (Customization → Layout →
   Featured sections → add "Podcasts" and move it to the top), then "Videos", then "Shorts".
6. Copy the playlist URL into the description template's ALL EPISODES line.

Audio-only distribution (Spotify, Apple) is a separate step through a podcast host and is **not**
covered by this plan; see open questions.

## 7. End screens and cards

**End screens** need 5–20 s of clean picture at the end of the video, so the editor adds a
**20-second outro** to every episode: the 16:9 sting (1.5 s), then a charcoal panel with the
medallion small at left, the website in gold text and the website's QR code at right, and the
sign-off as the last line. The end-screen elements sit on the open space, two of them centered.

| Episode | Element 1 (left) | Element 2 (right) | Element 3 (small) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ep 1 | Video: Ep 2 (set to "Best for viewer" until Ep 2 is uploaded, then edit to Ep 2) | Playlist: 4:19 Podcast | Subscribe (round, medallion) |
| Ep 2 | Video: Ep 3 | Playlist: 4:19 Podcast | Subscribe |
| Ep 3 (latest) | Video: "Best for viewer" | Playlist: 4:19 Podcast | Subscribe |
| Every Short | n/a (Shorts have no end screens); use the related-video link instead | | |

When a new episode publishes, open the previous episode's end screen and point element 1 at it.
Keep a copy of the layout with "Save as template" the first time; apply it to later uploads.

**Website link on screen:** an end-screen or card **link element** to an external site requires the
YouTube Partner Program (the channel will not have it at launch), so the site is reached through
the QR code and spoken plug in the outro, the description, the pinned comment and the channel
links block. That is enough; it is also the "natural, never a commercial" plug the team wants.

**Cards** (the "i" in the top-right during playback), one or two per episode:

- At the moment Larry mentions the ministry or a men's weekend: card → the podcast playlist
  (external links are blocked without YPP, so the playlist card stands in for the site here).
- On Ep 2 and Ep 3: card → the other part of "Six Months Later" at the first reference to it.
- On Ep 1 at the 1-minute mark: card → the channel trailer or the podcast playlist.
